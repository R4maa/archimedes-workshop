You don’t have to do this alone.
You have everyone you used to be.

A little help from your past

  1. Move with arrow keys / WASD, the direction buttons, or tap a neighboring tile. Every move or wait is one beat. Walking into a wall also spends a beat.
  2. Stand on a lettered plate to open every door with that letter. Doors read the positions before your next move.
  3. Press Record echo / R. Time rewinds. Your footsteps replay whenever you move, then your echo stays on its last tile.
  4. Echoes are memories: they pass through doors, overlap, and never block you. Reach the diamond exit with your present self.

Space waits · Z undoes a beat · recordings and progress stay on this device when storage is available. Nothing moves until you do.
